# WahlMate argument review — 14 September 2026

Reviewed all 63 current statements: Germany (2025 programme basis), Berlin and Mecklenburg-Vorpommern. Rewrote both sides in German and English (252 explanations) in dist/arguments.js. Each pair should explain a concrete benefit versus a concrete objection to the actual proposal. Uncertain outcomes use conditional language; these are editorial arguments, not authorised party answers.

Statements, party positions, ranking and share-link datasets are unchanged. Overrides require the current statement to match; historical statements retain their original explanations. This is an editorial review, not a new audit of party alignments.

Key corrections:
- Germany 2: earlier infrastructure repair and spreading costs over its use versus interest commitments and reallocating existing budgets.
- Germany 6: investment returns versus the transition funding gap and market risk.
- Berlin 19: convenience versus the cost of parallel digital/paper services; the proposal already retains paper access.
- Berlin 15: access to non-commercial culture versus the other public services that would lose funding.
- Berlin 1: acknowledges the earlier state rent cap's lack of legislative competence, without asserting that every possible future cap is impossible.
